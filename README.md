# LoopedIn: Your close-knit crafting community.
### Created by Emily Warren, Carlie Reynoso, Sualeha Irshad, Alec Holland, Madison Westbrook, & Sul Ha Yang

LoopedIn is a craft-focused app that was designed to help crafters and hobbyists connect with others, discover resources, and stay organized. The core features of LoopedIn allow users to track progress on projects, share those projects with their community, and support each other throughout the creative process.

Although some other crafting community platforms do exist, they are largely considered inadequate due to their unintuitive design, unclear/few features, and inaccessibility from a mobile device. Our app aims to resolve each of these problems to promote continued crafting and community.
